THE RUKMINI PHILOSOPHY

Tradition, tailored for today.

Located in the heart of Prasadampadu, Vijayawada, Rukmini Sarees was founded with a singular passion: celebrating the timeless grace of Indian textiles while offering a modern, welcoming boutique shopping experience.

Our bridal and festive collections are curated directly from renowned master weavers, ensuring genuine silk authenticity, intricate zari motifs, and long-lasting lustre.

Beyond sarees, we believe that styling a saree is an art form. From finding the exact matching raw silk blouse piece to color-coordinated lining fabrics, falls, and 2M/3M lengths, our dedicated in-store team helps you complete your ensemble with effortless confidence.
