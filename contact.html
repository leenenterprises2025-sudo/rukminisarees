Where exactly is the store located?

We are conveniently situated right near Kasturibhai School in Prasadampadu, Vijayawada - 521108. Ample two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking is available nearby.

Can I bring a saree to find matching lining and blouse pieces?

Yes, absolutely! We specialize in color matching. Bring your saree or a swatch, and our experienced staff will help you find the exact shade in blouse materials, lining, 2M/3M cuts, and borders.
